# 0003. Use AI to support learning

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-01

## Context

AI tools has become a regular part of development, including at work, where its essentially a requirement. This repository is also a place to learn. Relying on *vibes* (AI) to do the work for me could make that learning less effective.

## Decision 

Use AI in this repository for rubber-ducking and discussing complex design choices. Avoid delegating work I need to understand in order to maintain the setup.

## Consequences

- Things may take some more time, but at least I'll understand everything being set up.
